Class Clock is the phone app for BSIT 2-1. It keeps the weekly schedule, class countdown, and task deadlines on the device. Schedule changes, skipped classes, shifts, and no-class days are checked from the same Class Clock schedule used on this site.

FAQ
Install, scanning, iOS, bugs, and Google Play
Is this app safe to install?

Yes. The Android file on this page is Class Clock, posted here for BSIT 2-1. Download it only from this site. It uses the schedule on this site, plus notifications and alarms for class and task times. It is installed directly, not from the Play Store.

Play Protect says that the app needs to be scanned first.

That message is normal. Play Protect scans apps that are not installed from the Play Store. Let the scan finish, then continue the install. Class Clock is the APK linked on this page.

Why is the app not available yet for iOS?

There is no iPhone build yet. The download on this page is Android only, so the iOS button stays marked as not yet supported.
